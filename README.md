# 🚀 Not Enough Space — Abandoned Space Junk by NASA

A **comic + trivia adventure for ages 7–12**, made for the
**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** challenge
*"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment
on the Moon and Mars"*.

Kids read a comic chapter about real NASA hardware left on the Moon, Mars and
in deep space, then play **The Deep Space Dive** — an open-ended quiz where
*rarer answers sink you deeper* — to unlock the next comic.

## 🌌 Why "Not Enough Space"?

Space looks endless — but the places we can actually reach are not. Every
mission leaves something behind on the Moon and Mars: landers, rovers,
science packages, descent stages, and pieces of spacecraft that never came
home. Little by little, the quiet, empty surfaces we once aimed for are
becoming crowded with our own leftovers.

So there is still plenty of *space* out there — but less and less of it is
untouched. The name is a playful reminder that we are already filling space
up, and that what we leave behind deserves to be remembered rather than
forgotten.

That is what this app is about: each comic chapter brings one more piece of
abandoned hardware back into the story, until the "junk" starts to look like
a museum of everything we have dared to try.

## ▶ How it works

**Read a comic chapter → win The Deep Space Dive → unlock the next chapter.**
You can replay any chapter at any time to earn a better depth or more stars.

- **14 chapters** — The Moon (5) → Mars (6) → Deep Space (3)
- Your progress saves automatically on your device — **no account needed, and
  nothing is ever sent anywhere** (kid-privacy friendly)
- Earn up to **3 stars per chapter**: 🌊 reach the goal depth (pass), dive
  deeper (★2), reach the deep zone (★3). Each chapter also hides one 💎
  **"One in a Deep Space"** gem answer.
- Every star is genuinely achievable — **all 42 stars** can be earned, so no
  badge is ever a dead end.
- Finish all 14 missions to unlock a personalized, printable
  **Explorer Certificate**.

## 🎮 The Deep Space Dive

The game idea is inspired by [krillion.io](https://krillion.io/):

1. You get an open question, for example *"What part of the Lunar Module was
   left behind on the Moon?"*
2. Type **any** answer and press **🤿 DIVE** — typos and near-misses are
   forgiving. If a typo is closer to a rarer answer, you still get credit for
   that rarer one.
3. **Rarer answers = deeper.** Common answers give a little depth; deep cuts
   give lots; the 💎 gem gives a huge splash.
4. Reach the goal depth to unlock the next comic. Fall short? No problem —
   retry as often as you like.
5. **⏱️ The timer is your choice** — Relaxed 40 s / Normal 25 s / Quick 15 s,
   or no timer at all. Pick it before a dive or in Settings.
6. **💡 Hints** show the easy answers as tappable chips, and every round ends
   with a debrief listing all accepted answers plus the official NASA facts —
   so you always learn something, even when you don't win.
7. **🌊 Depth zones** — the dive is a journey, not a score: *Starfish
   Shallows → The Moon Belt → Comet Trail → The Deep Dark → Ice Cold Orbit →
   Planet Belt → The Far Side*.

## ✨ What's inside

- **🛰️ Space Junk Museum** — 13 real equipment exhibits; each lights up when
  you finish its chapter
- **🛠️ Space Junk Rescue** — spend starlight earned from your stars to restore
  derelicts; restored exhibits light up green in the Museum
- **🔭 Mission Control** — browse real spacecraft records from NASA's archive
- **📔 My Collection Album** — all 13 exhibits as collectable cards with
  progress rings
- **⚖️ Compare two derelicts** — put any two spacecraft side by side
- **🏅 11 achievements** — Story Starter, First Plunge, Gem Hunter, Deep Diver,
  Moon Explorer, Mars Explorer, Deep-Space Voyager, Grand Champion, Space
  Historian, Space Junk Rescue, Archive Diver
- **🏆 Explorer Certificate** — personalized with your Explorer Name, ready
  to print
- **👨‍👩‍👧 Grown-up progress report** — a printable one-pager for parents
- **🌟 Explorer Name** — optional, stays on your device only, and greets you
  on the home screen
- **📤 Export / 📥 Import progress** — move your save to another device
- **📲 Add to Home Screen** — install it like an app and play **fully offline**,
  even with no internet
- **🔊 Friendly read-aloud** — a natural-sounding voice with warm pacing and
  real pauses, on the home screen, every comic page, the questions, the facts
  and every museum exhibit
- **🌍 English / Español** — the whole site, including the dive and the museum
- **♿ Accessibility options** — less motion, high contrast, easy-read font and
  text size (90–130%), plus keyboard and screen-reader support
- **ℹ️ About & Sources** — every screen has a footer link to a page explaining
  where the facts come from, how privacy works, who made it, and credits

## 🧒 Made for kids

- Big rounded buttons, large type, high contrast and a friendly rounded font
  that keeps working even with no internet
- Read-aloud works offline and nothing is sent anywhere
- Sound effects with an on/off switch and a volume slider, plus a
  "Less motion" setting
- Keyboard support: ← → to turn comic pages, Enter to submit, Esc to close
  Settings
- Works beautifully on phones and tablets — big tap targets, swipe to turn
  comic pages, and no awkward zooming
- No dead ends: locked chapters explain exactly what to finish first, and every
  message is a friendly in-app one

Every piece of equipment and every fact in the app comes from NASA's own
records and the official Space Apps 2026 equipment list, so what kids learn is
real.
